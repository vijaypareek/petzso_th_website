What is PetzSo?

PetzSo is an app that helps pet parents find trusted pet services, pet friendly places, events, and all essentials nearby.
Everything is in one place, so you don’t have to depend on unverified info, random searches, WhatsApp groups, or scattered information.

Find best for your Pet

Discover nearby vets, groomers, trainers, walkers, boarding services, and more — verified and easy to contact or book.

Book Appointments Easily

Check availability, view details, and book pet services directly from the app — no calls, no confusion.

Explore Pet-Friendly Places & Events

Find cafés, parks, events, and hangout spots where pets are truly welcome — curated for your city.

About the Founders ❤️

Founded by Vijay & Prashant, two friends and fellow pet parents building the app they wish existed. After struggling to find pet-friendly places, reliable pet services and trustworthy local information in one platform, they decided to create PetzSo to solve these problems for pet families everywhere.

Vijay

Co-founder · Product & Community

Pet dad Vijay spent the past year exploring pet-friendly places and activities around the city with his wife and energetic puppy. That ongoing journey inspired PetzSo. Today he leads our product design, user experience and community building, making sure the app is truly useful for urban pet parents.
