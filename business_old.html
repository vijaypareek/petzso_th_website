To keep listings accurate and trustworthy, we may ask for business proof before approving a claim or activating a profile.

Business proof

Depending on the business type, this may include one or more of these:

  • Registration, license, or GST document
  • Business card or branded invoice
  • Business lease agteement or owner's ID card
📱

Contact confirmation

We may confirm the number or contact details linked to the business.

  • Phone or WhatsApp number
  • Official business email (if available)
🌐

Visible business presence

If formal documents are not available, these can also help support verification.

  • Instagram or social profile
  • Business profile on Google (if available)
  • Any other legal document as needed

FAQ

Quick answers before you claim or set up your business.

My business is already listed on PetzSo. How do I claim it?

Contact us on WhatsApp or LINE with your business name, phone number and location. Our team will review the request, ask for proof if needed, and guide you through the process.

I am new to PetzSo. Can you still help me set up my business?

Yes. If you are new, we can guide you through the setup process and help you understand how to create and manage your profile on PetzSo Partner.

What proof do I need to claim a business?

Depending on your business type, we may ask for a registration, any legal document or another visible proof that proves authenticity of the business.

Can I update my profile later?

Yes. Once your business is activated properly, you can update photos, hours, services, products, and contact details through PetzSo Partner.
