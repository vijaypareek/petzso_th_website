What is PetzSo?

PetzSo is an app that helps pet parents find trusted pet services, pet-friendly places, events, and essentials nearby. Everything is in one place, so you don’t have to rely on random searches, scattered information, or unverified recommendations.

Find trusted pet services

Discover nearby vets, groomers, trainers, walkers, boarding services, and more — easy to contact and simple to compare.

Book appointments easily

Check details, view availability, and book pet services directly through the app — without the usual back and forth.

Explore places and events

Find pet-friendly cafés, parks, events, and hangout spots near you — all curated for your city.
