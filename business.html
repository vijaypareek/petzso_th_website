What we may ask for

To keep business listings accurate and trustworthy, we may ask for proof before approving a claim or activating a profile.

🪪

Business proof

Depending on your business type, this may include one or more of these:

  • Registration, license, or GST document
  • Business card or branded invoice
  • Business lease agreement or owner ID
📱

Contact confirmation

We may confirm the contact details linked to the business.

  • Phone or WhatsApp number
  • Official business email, if available
🌐

Visible business presence

If formal documents are not available, these can also help support verification.

  • Instagram or other social profile
  • Google business presence, if available
  • Any other valid proof as needed

FAQ

Quick answers before you claim or set up your business.

My business is already listed on PetzSo. How do I claim it?

Send the request form with your business name, city, and phone number. Our team will review it, ask for proof if needed, and guide you through the next step.

My business is not listed yet. Can I still join?

Yes. If your business is not listed yet, we can help create your listing first and then help you claim and manage it properly.

Do I need to download the app to get started?

No. You can start on web through this request form. Once your listing is ready, you can use PetzSo Partner to manage your business faster.

What if someone else tries to claim my business?

We review claims carefully and may ask for proof before approving changes. This helps keep listings safe and accurate.

I need help right now. How can I contact you?

You can contact us on WhatsApp, LINE, or email. Our support details are given below.

Need help before sending the form?

If your case is urgent or you are not sure what to select in the form, contact our team directly and we will guide you.
